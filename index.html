01 / About

Strategy-minded.
Hands-on by nature.

I’m a web and e-commerce specialist with 15+ years across design, development, operations, technical support, and team leadership.

I’m at my best when I can own the full build: get clear on the goal, shape the customer journey, design and code the experience, then get it launched and working. When the brief has gaps, I ask, research, document assumptions, and bring options back for alignment rather than quietly guessing. After launch, I like to learn from analytics and feedback and keep improving the experience.
